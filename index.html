Lab 1 · 30 min

Build your brand kit in Canva

30:00
  1. Write your positioning statement and choose 3 voice words from the sliders activity.
  2. Pick a palette on coolors.co: 1 primary, 1 accent, 1 neutral. Copy the hex codes.
  3. In Canva, search “logo”, choose a simple template and change it to your colours and fonts. Keep it readable at small sizes.
  4. Create a page called Brand Kit with logo, hex codes, heading font, body font and voice words. Canva Pro users can save these in Brand Kit; free users keep this page.
  5. Design 3 reusable post templates: a product post, a quote or tip post and a customer review post.

Lecture 2 · Activity · ask “So what?”

Features tell. Benefits sell.

A feature is what the product has. A benefit is what the customer gets. Read the feature, let the class shout “So what?”, then flip the card.

Your turn (pairs, 4 min): write 3 features of your own business. Your partner asks “So what?” until you reach a real benefit.

    Blogging Strategy

    By the end you can

    • Plan a blog around content pillars and topic clusters
    • Turn a keyword into a structured blog outline
    • Balance helpful posts and promotional posts
    • Build a 4-week content calendar

    Today's lab

    A blog plan: 1 pillar topic, 4 cluster posts, one full outline and a 4-week calendar.

    Tools: your Module 4 keyword sheet, Google “People also ask”, ChatGPT, Google Sheets

    Lecture 3 · Concept · click any post

    One big guide, many small posts, all linked

    Pillar + cluster

    A pillar page covers a broad topic. Cluster posts each answer one specific question and link back to the pillar. Google sees you as an expert on the whole topic.

    Pick 3–4 content pillars for your business. For Sweet Oven: Cake guides Recipes & tips Celebrations Behind the scenes
    Lecture 3 · Hands-on · 8 min

    Turn one keyword into a blog outline

    Pick a post type and type a keyword. The outline shows the structure Google and readers expect. You fill in the words.

    Every post needs
    • Keyword in the title, first paragraph and one H2
    • An answer in the first 100 words
    • 2 or more links to your own posts and one to a trusted outside source
    • One image every 300 words or so, with alt text
    • A clear next step at the end
    Lecture 3 · Interactive

    Plan a month: help first, sell second

    Click any slot to change its pillar. Aim for no more than 1 promotional post in every 4. If every post sells, people stop listening.

    Your content mix

    Lab 3 · 30 min

    Plan your blog and your month

    30:00
    1. Open your Module 4 keyword sheet. Pick the broadest keyword as your pillar topic.
    2. Search it on Google. Copy 4 questions from People also ask. Each one becomes a cluster post.
    3. Write a full outline for one cluster post: title, intro answer, 4–6 H2s, internal links and a call to action.
    4. Build a 4-week calendar in Google Sheets with columns: date, platform, pillar, topic, format, status.
    5. No website yet? Publish the post on a free platform such as WordPress.com, Blogger or Medium.

      Lab 4 · 35 min

      Send your first email campaign

      35:00
      1. Create a free Mailchimp account. Add your business details; every email must show a real contact address.
      2. Set up your Audience and create a hosted signup form in your brand colours. Add a lead magnet line, such as “Get our free cake size chart”.
      3. Swap signup links with 5 classmates and subscribe to each other's lists. Only add people who agreed.
      4. Build a welcome email from a template: logo, greeting, what to expect, the lead magnet and one button.
      5. Send a test to yourself and check it on your phone. Then send to your list and watch the report together next class.
